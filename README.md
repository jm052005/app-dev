# My favorite series is Dexter

## Dexter Morgan is the main character of the popular crime-drama television franchise Dexter.

*Dexter Morgan is a fictional Miami police blood-spatter analyst who secretly operates as a vigilante serial killer targeting murderers who have escaped the legal system. He manages his violent urges using a strict moral code taught by his adoptive father, ensuring he only kills guilty people while maintaining a normal public life.*
